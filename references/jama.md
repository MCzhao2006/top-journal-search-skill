# JAMA Adapter

## Scope

Unqualified `JAMA` means the flagship journal. `JAMA Network` expands to specialty journals and JAMA Network Open.

Official domain: `jamanetwork.com`.

## Search behavior

JAMA Network provides simple and advanced search. Search results can be narrowed by publication, collection, article/content type, and date range, and sorted by relevance/date. Advanced search supports author, date range, article type, publication, keyword, title, citation, and DOI fields.

## JAMA-specific verification

- Verify flagship `JAMA` vs JAMA specialty journal / JAMA Network Open.
- Record article type (Original Investigation, Research Letter, Review, Guideline/Recommendation, Viewpoint, etc.).
- For practice-impact claims, distinguish original research from recommendations, editorials, and news/commentary.
- JAMA DOI patterns commonly begin `10.1001/`.
