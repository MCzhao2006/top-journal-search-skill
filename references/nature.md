# Nature Adapter

## Scope

Unqualified `Nature` means the flagship journal. Expand to Nature Portfolio only when requested.

Official domain: `nature.com`.

## Search behavior

Nature's site supports basic search by keywords, phrases, article title, author, and DOI, plus advanced search with journal and date filtering. It also supports filtering search results by article type, journal, date, and sorting by relevance/date.

Useful starting patterns:

- broad site search: `https://www.nature.com/search?q=<query>`
- flagship browse: `https://www.nature.com/nature/articles`

Do not assume Nature's online archive is exhaustive for very old material; use PubMed/Crossref for discovery and then verify available publisher records.

## Nature-specific verification

- Confirm that the article is actually in `Nature`, not merely another Nature Portfolio title.
- Distinguish research articles from News, News & Views, Editorials, Reviews, Correspondence, and Research Highlights.
- For “latest research”, prefer research-article filters and actual publication dates.
