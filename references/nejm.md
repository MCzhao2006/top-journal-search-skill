# NEJM Adapter

## Scope

Default to `The New England Journal of Medicine` (`NEJM`), not other NEJM Group products unless requested.

Official domain: `nejm.org`.

## Search behavior

NEJM supports general and advanced search across its archive, including title/abstract/full-article terms, authors, article category, and date ranges. Search results can be filtered/sorted by article type/category, specialty, date, newest/oldest, and citation/view metrics.

NEJM search supports quoted phrases, wildcard `*`, and Boolean operators `AND`, `OR`, `NOT` with parentheses. Use these features when a basic keyword search is noisy.

## NEJM-specific verification

- Confirm article category: Original Article, Review Article, Perspective, Editorial, Correspondence, Images, etc.
- For clinical-trial claims, verify study design and primary endpoint before summarizing impact.
- NEJM DOI patterns commonly begin `10.1056/`, but the article page remains the authority for journal identity.
