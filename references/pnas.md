# PNAS Adapter

## Scope

Default to `Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)`. Include `PNAS Nexus` only if requested or clearly relevant.

Official domain: `pnas.org`.

## Search behavior

PNAS provides basic search plus Advanced Search at:

`https://www.pnas.org/search/advanced`

Advanced Search supports date/year, abstract keywords, access status, and publication selection (including PNAS vs PNAS Nexus). Results can be refined by author, publication, keywords/topics, and article type and sorted by relevance/date/most viewed/most cited.

## PNAS-specific verification

- Confirm PNAS vs PNAS Nexus.
- Check whether the item is a Research Article, Commentary, Perspective, Letter, Correction, etc.
- The archive reaches back to 1915; for historical searches, date filters and exact-title lookup are especially useful.
- PNAS DOI patterns commonly begin `10.1073/`.
