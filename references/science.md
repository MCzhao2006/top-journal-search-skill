# Science (AAAS) Adapter

## Scope

Unqualified `Science` means the flagship `Science` journal. Do not silently include Science Advances, Science Translational Medicine, Science Immunology, Science Robotics, Science Signaling, or other AAAS titles.

Official domain: `science.org`.

## Search behavior

Prefer the Science search interface / Advanced Search and then open the article page. A commonly used direct search pattern is:

`https://www.science.org/action/doSearch?AllField=<query>`

An Advanced Search entry point is available under the Science search interface. If direct search URLs stop working, use the site's search UI or a domain-restricted web query instead of assuming the old URL contract is stable.

## Science-specific verification

- Confirm journal identity in the article header.
- Distinguish Research Articles/Reports from News, Perspectives, Editorials, Policy Forums, Letters, and other content.
- Science DOI patterns commonly begin `10.1126/`, but verify the exact journal.
